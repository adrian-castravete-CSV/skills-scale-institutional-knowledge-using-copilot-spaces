# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality strategy and test coverage for features and releases. They coordinate testing efforts, define acceptance criteria validation approaches, and ensure products meet quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plans aligned with feature scope
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Work with Developers to identify testability gaps and design improvements
- Validate acceptance criteria are met before marking work complete
- Identify and escalate quality risks and blockers
- Conduct post-release smoke tests and regression testing
- Track and report on test coverage and quality metrics

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal defects in production
- Reduce cycle time by identifying issues early in development
- Maintain customer confidence through rigorous quality validation
- Build a quality-first culture across the delivery team

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and refinement discussions
- Daily standups to flag testing blockers and progress
- Test reports and quality dashboards
- Collaboration with developers on test automation and edge cases
- Post-release incident reviews and retrospectives

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Partner on testability, test automation, and design decisions
- **Project Manager**: Report on quality risks and testing readiness for releases
- **Product Manager**: Collaborate on acceptance criteria clarity and testing priorities

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define the system design, technology choices, and technical direction for projects. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational standards and constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and technical approach for features
- Make recommendations on technology choices and trade-offs
- Ensure solutions follow security, performance, and scalability standards
- Review designs for technical feasibility and risk
- Identify technical dependencies and integration points
- Mentor developers on architectural patterns and best practices
- Contribute to technical decision documentation

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable systems
- Reduce technical debt and complexity
- Ensure long-term supportability and evolvability
- Align technology decisions with organizational strategy

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical design documents and decision records (ADRs)
- Sprint planning to assess technical feasibility
- Cross-team architecture alignment meetings
- Knowledge sharing sessions and code reviews

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Guide on technical decisions and architectural patterns
- **Project Manager**: Advise on technical risks and dependencies
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Collaborate on testability and quality requirements

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads advocate for user experience and usability. They validate design decisions against user needs, ensure consistency with design standards, and champion customer-first delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and gather feedback on features
- Define interaction flows and visual designs
- Validate designs meet usability and accessibility standards
- Review implementation for fidelity to design intent
- Identify and escalate UX risks and gaps
- Maintain design systems and consistency guidelines
- Participate in user acceptance testing and feedback collection

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, user-friendly products
- Reduce support burden through good UX
- Build customer satisfaction and loyalty
- Align product experience with brand standards

### Typical Communication
- Design collaboration sessions with Product and Developers
- Design reviews and feedback cycles
- User research findings and insights
- Usability testing sessions and reports
- Sprint refinement to discuss user stories and acceptance criteria

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Collaborate on user needs and feature prioritization
- **Developers**: Partner on implementation details and design feasibility
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Define UX test scenarios and acceptance criteria

---

## Sponsor / Business Owner

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide strategic direction, business context, and executive support. They approve scope and budget, champion the project, and ensure alignment with organizational objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Approve project scope and budget
- Champion the project and remove organizational blockers
- Participate in key decision gates (go/no-go)
- Provide strategic feedback and course correction
- Communicate project status and value to broader organization
- Escalate and resolve business-level conflicts

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value and ROI
- Maintain executive alignment and support
- Reduce organizational friction and blockers
- Enable rapid decision-making at the strategic level

### Typical Communication
- Monthly executive updates and business reviews
- Key milestone approvals and decision gates
- Escalation and issue resolution forums
- All-hands announcements and project launches

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Manager**: Escalation point for business-level blockers and decisions
- **Product Manager**: Collaborate on business objectives and success metrics
- **Developers**: Understand technical trade-offs affecting timeline/cost

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Delivery Coaches facilitate agile practices, remove process blockers, and coach teams on continuous improvement. They serve the team and help optimize delivery workflows.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, standups, retrospectives, reviews)
- Remove blockers preventing team progress
- Coach team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track and report on team velocity and burndown metrics
- Identify process bottlenecks and recommend improvements
- Foster psychological safety and team collaboration
- Escalate systemic issues and dependencies

### Goals
- Enable team to deliver at sustainable pace
- Continuously improve delivery processes and team dynamics
- Maximize team autonomy and decision-making
- Reduce cycle time and unplanned work

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective findings and action items
- Metrics dashboards and process improvement updates
- One-on-ones with team members
- Cross-team dependency and escalation management

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **All Roles**: Facilitate collaboration, remove blockers, enable effectiveness
- **Project Manager**: Coordinate on dependencies and schedule impacts
- **Team Members**: Coach on process adherence and continuous improvement
